Life

You are a compy, a dinosaur about the size of a chicken, trying to make it in the big town. Get a job, try not to get fired, stay out of jail, find a home, fall in love, hatch some eggs, and pray no tree lands on your roof.

Move with WASD or arrows. Walk up to a glowing door and press E to go inside any building. Press E next to a car to drive it (H honks). Buy your own at Dino Motors, or steal one if you dare (half the time nobody sees). Weapons from the black market: F to use, Q to switch. On a phone, drag on the left to move and tap ENTER.
