# Emberfall Chronicles

A browser action RPG. Every sprite, tile and effect is drawn in code on a canvas, so there are no image assets. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- 8 regions with fog-of-war and a day/night cycle
- Farming and cooking: a six-plot garden beside Willow Cottage. Buy seeds from Tess, plant crops that only grow in their seasons (turnips, carrots, wheat, spring strawberries, summer tomatoes, autumn pumpkins and winter frostberries), water them every half-day (rain does it for you) and harvest them before their season ends or they wither. Cook them at a campfire or the pot by your garden into meals with buffs: Garden Stew (regeneration), Fresh Bread (defense), Strawberry Tart (speed), Tomato Soup (mana and faster cooldowns), Pumpkin Pie (attack), Frostberry Jam (damage reduction and slow immunity) and the Harvest Feast (a bit of everything). Every festival holds a cooking contest on the village square: enter your best dish against Ned, Tess and Borin, and win gold, an Elixir of Might and a Cooking Trophy for your home
- Seasons and weather: spring, summer, autumn and winter each last three in-game days and change the world (blossoming trees and falling petals, golden summer light, red and gold autumn leaves, snow-capped trees and drifting snow). Weather shifts between clear skies, rain, snow, fog and thunderstorms: rain weakens fire creatures and emboldens water creatures, snow slows everyone and strengthens frost creatures, fog hides you until monsters are close, and thunderstorms rain lightning down on hero and monster alike while storm creatures grow stronger. Each season has its own monster (Bloomlings, Giant Dragonflies, Leaflings hiding in leaf piles, snowball-throwing Snow Golems) and fish (Blossom Koi, Sunfish, Maplefin, Icefin), Storm Wisps appear only in thunderstorms, and the first day of every season brings a village festival with bunting, lanterns, a gift basket and a bonus (Blossom Festival, Midsummer Fair, Harvest Festival, Frostfest)

Controls: Esc or the Pause button to pause (the game also pauses itself when you switch tabs), WASD to move, Space or click to attack, F or right-click for your skill, Shift to dash, X to ride, P for the stable, 1/2/3 to cast spells, B for the spellbook, E to interact, Q/R for potions, I for your bag and weapons, T for talents, C to decorate your house, G to throw a bomb, V to deploy a ranged unit, Enter to chat, O for the player list, L for the journal, M for the map.
