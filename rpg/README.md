# Emberfall Chronicles

A browser action RPG. Every sprite, tile and effect is drawn in code on a canvas, so there are no image assets. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- 15 classes: Warrior (Whirlwind), Mage (Fireball), Ranger (Arrow Storm), Rogue (Shadowstep backstab), Paladin (Holy Smite, stuns and heals), Necromancer (Raise Dead skeleton army), Monk (Flurry, a dash that strikes everything in its path), Druid (Bramble, a thorn patch that poisons and slows enemies and heals you) Engineer (Turret, an auto-firing crossbow turret), Berserker (Rampage, a leaping slam that sends you into a rage), Bard (Battle Hymn, a stunning sound wave that inspires you to attack faster and heal) Shaman (Storm Totem, a lightning totem that also heals you), Samurai (Iai Strike, one enormous forward slash), Gunslinger (Fan the Hammer, a rapid six-shot burst) and Alchemist (Volatile Flask, a lingering cloud of fire, frost or poison)
- A talent tree with five shared branches (Might, Guard, Arcana, Mastery for weapon-type damage and attack speed, and Survival, which boosts gathering, potions, pets, mounts and chest gold), each with a powerful tier-4 capstone, plus a four-tier branch unique to each class that upgrades its signature skill and ends in a game-changing capstone (split fireballs, multishot, exploding skeletons, twice-as-fast turrets and more); points come from levels and story chapters, and talents can be reset for gold
- 77 weapons any class can use (flintlock pistols, twin flintlocks, a piercing long musket, a blunderbuss, a rapid-fire brass repeater, swords, greatswords, katana, daggers, spears, scythe, greataxes, mauls, flail, staffs, wands, bows, crossbows, a scattershot crossbow, throwing knives, exploding fire staffs, a lunging rapier, spinning twin glaives, a ricochet disc that bounces between enemies, a rocket-firing hand cannon, a life-stealing bloodstaff, a three-way prism staff, blowgun, boomerang, chakram, and the legendary Starforged Blade for conquering every dungeon), with burn, freeze, poison, stun, lifesteal, execute, chain lightning, piercing and raise-the-dead effects; your class gets +15% with its own weapon type
- 33 spells taught by Elder Mira, including Thunderstorm, Phantom Blades (orbiting swords), Blizzard, Time Warp, Divine Shield, Earthen Spikes, Lightning Dash, Summon Imps, Mend, Rejuvenate, Blink, Thunderstrike, Soul Siphon, Ice Prison, Sanctuary, Singularity (a black hole), Stone Guardian, Battle Frenzy, Frost Nova, Chain Lightning, Glacial Spike, Arcane Barrier, Vampiric Touch, Wall of Flames, Earthquake, Cyclone, Spirit Wolves, Meteor, Starfall and Recall, cast from slots 1-3
- 8 regions with fog-of-war, a day/night cycle and rain
- 45 monster types, including spitting toads, blinking wisps, lunging spiders and bandits, magma slimes that split in two, root-casting treants, diving harpies, poison-stinging ash scorpions, spore-cloud mushrooms, snowball-throwing yetis, ghouls, mimics that pretend to be treasure chests, burning Ember Knights, blinking Shades and Void Cultists, charging boars, gold-stealing pixies, spear-throwing Marsh Lurkers, burrowing Dune Worms, gargoyles that pose as statues until you get close, Drowned Sailors, exploding Sporelings, poisonous Mycoids, Clockwork Soldiers, Spiderbots, Sentry Turrets, regenerating Bog Trolls (burn them to stop it), fire-trailing Salamanders, Storm Ravens, night-only Werewolves, wailing Banshees and Lunar Moths, Crystal Crawlers, Shardlings, laser-sighted Powder Gunners and Bomb Throwers
- 15 bosses: Varg the Bandit King (bandit camp), the Hollow King (Sunken Ruins), the Ashen Wyrm (Ashen Wastes), Morwen, the Moon Witch (a night-only boss in a ring of glowing standing stones in the eastern meadows: homing moon bolts, chasing moonbeams, a hex that weakens you, teleports, moonfall and raven summons; drops the Staff of the Pale Moon, stronger at night, and Corvin the raven), Captain Blackbrine (a ghost pirate at the shipwreck on the southern coast: cannon barrages, a spinning cutlass, a boomerang anchor, ghostly dashes and a drowned crew; drops Blackbrine's Cutlass and Polly the parrot), Sylvara, the Blighted Heart (a dead grove in the western Whisperwood: root lines, spore clouds, thorn storms and treant summons; drops the Heartwood Bow and the Twig pet), and one at the bottom of each dungeon
- 9 dungeons with rooms, corridors, torches, treasure and monster packs: the Mossy Crypt (Arachne, the Brood Queen), the Drowned Temple (Nerith, the Tide Serpent), the Frostfang Caverns (Glacius, the Frost Titan), the lava-filled Ashforge Depths (Pyrrhus, the Forgemaster: molten slams, fire spins and a burning charge) the endgame Void Rift (Malgrath, the Void King: seeking orbs, teleport novas, shadow pillars and a bullet-hell spiral), Mycelium Hollow by Mirror Lake (Mycelia, the Spore Mother: spore clouds, mycelium lines, spore rain and sporeling swarms) Crystal Hollow (Prismara, the Crystal Queen, who hides behind a shield until you break her Prism Nodes), Blackpowder Keep (Baron Gunnar, the Cannon King: musket volleys, mortar strikes, exploding powder kegs, a shield charge and a gatling sweep) and the Clockwork Vault (the Brass Colossus: a sweeping laser that leaves it overheated and vulnerable, homing rockets, shockwave rings and drones). Dungeon bosses can be fought again on every visit. Chapters VI and VII of the story lead you through the Ashforge Depths, the Void Rift and the Clockwork Vault
- A story in 8 chapters with illustrated cinematics (prologue, epilogue and a grand finale) and spoken dialogue scenes: recover Seraphine's map from Varg, gather the three Emberstone shards, reforge the stone and slay the Ashen Wyrm; then face Pyrrhus and the Void King, break into the Clockwork Vault, and finally light Seraphine's three beacons and perform the ritual at her ruined tower to bring the archmage home for the Festival of Stars
- 74 quests (19 main story, 55 side quests) from 9 characters, including Captain Rhea's bounties, Orrin the scholar's lore hunts, Wren the wandering bard's six-part ballad (which ends with Wren's Lyre), and Seraphine herself once she returns (who gives you Celeste, the star stag); with a gold arrow that points to your tracked quest
- 6 waystones for fast travel and 8 lost pages of Seraphine's journal to find
- 20 achievements and a Bestiary
- Fishing minigame, mining, woodcutting and foraging, each with its own skill level
- Forge (5 weapon and 5 armor tiers), potion brewing, campfire cooking
- A workbench and Crafting skill: 30+ recipes for furniture, rings and amulets (equippable, with stat bonuses), bombs, whetstones and tonics
- Every house in Emberfall can be entered: Elder Mira's house, Borin's smithy (use his forges), Lin's cottage (use her cauldrons), the Rusty Tankard Inn (chat with Hollis the barkeep, sleep in the spare bed) and Grandma Petra's house (her pie fully restores you)
- Willow Cottage, a house you can buy and decorate: place furniture, sleep for a Well Rested XP buff that grows with comfort, keep a storage chest, and mount boss trophies for bonus damage
- 39 pets with abilities (loot magnet, luck, healing, coin and material finding, life-stealing bites, slowing, burning and poison attacks, a phoenix that revives you, a corgi that boosts your attack, a hedgehog that pokes attackers, a harpy chick that speeds up your dash, a baby mimic that coughs up treasure, a glowing moon moth, a truffle-hunting piglet, a stony baby gargoyle, a pixie that sprinkles speed dust, a clockwork puppy that stuns, a fish-hoarding hamster, a dragonfly and a fiery star-fox): bought, adopted, fished up, found as rare drops, or won from bosses and the Arena
- 28 mounts, from a pony to a trampling sabertooth, a sturdy cave bear, a woolly mammoth, a giant war boar, a clockwork steed, a golden lion, a slow-but-sturdy giant snail, a lightning-fast raptor, a star stag, a healing unicorn and moss elk, a stealthy shadow panther, a burning magma hound, a swimming sea turtle and five fliers (drake, griffin, a giant moon moth, a stone gargoyle and a sky manta)
- Shop with goods, weapons, pets and mounts; 14 treasure chests
- Online multiplayer when published as a claude.ai artifact with the `room` capability: everyone who has the game open at once sees each other's heroes (class, gear, mount, name) and can chat; offline and in the standalone file the game is single-player
- The Emberfall Arena: a PvP zone where online players can fight each other, plus an 8-rank gladiator ladder against computer opponents (works offline) with a token shop
- Autosaves to the browser; plays with keyboard and mouse or touch

- Craftable ranged units at the workbench: Archer and Musketeer contracts hire allies who follow you and shoot, and a Field Cannon fires exploding cannonballs; press V to deploy one

Controls: WASD to move, Space or click to attack, F or right-click for your skill, Shift to dash, X to ride, P for the stable, 1/2/3 to cast spells, B for the spellbook, E to interact, Q/R for potions, I for your bag and weapons, T for talents, C to decorate your house, G to throw a bomb, V to deploy a ranged unit, Enter to chat, O for the player list, L for the journal, M for the map.

`game.html` is the source. Run `./build.sh` to rebuild `index.html` from it.
