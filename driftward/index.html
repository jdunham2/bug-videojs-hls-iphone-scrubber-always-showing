Your ship is stranded on an uncharted world with empty thrusters. Mine what the planet gives you, get airborne, hunt for Aurium, fight off pirates, salvage crashed ships, and jump to the next star. Every system, planet, creature and craft is generated from a seed, and the road never ends.
