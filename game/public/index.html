How to play

Emberhold is the last city. Explore the valley, find hidden chests, and step through the orange portal to delve into the Dungeon. Slay the Bone Tyrant to clear it — every clear makes enemies and loot stronger.

The violet portal leads to the Dark World, where the Hollow Merchant sells powerful gear for gold.

Ring the Night Bell beside the Ember Heart to start Defend the City. Press B to build barricades, towers, braziers and frost totems. Survive 5 waves for a big gold payout.
