How to play

Emberhold is the last city. Explore the valley, find hidden chests, and step through the orange portal to delve into the Dungeon. Dungeons rotate between the Bone Crypts, the Molten Forge (lava!) and the Frozen Catacombs (slippery ice). Pull the levers in the order shown on the boss gate, then slay the boss. Watch out for treasure goblins, elite monsters and spike traps, and use shrines for buffs.

The violet portal leads to the Dark World merchant. The Trainer resets talents; the Stash is shared by all your heroes. Your progress is saved on the server.

Ring the Night Bell to Defend the City. Build walls to reroute the horde, upgrade structures (U) and repair them (R) in build mode. The Siege Ogre arrives on wave 5.

Dodge-roll through attacks at the last moment for a Perfect Dodge. Lock on with V or middle mouse. Mix up your skills to raise your Style rank for bonus damage and gold. Walk up to a party member and press E to trade. Hold T for emotes.
