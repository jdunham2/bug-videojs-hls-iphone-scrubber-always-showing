Run, stomp and collect across three worlds, sell your finds in the village, fix up your home, then take back the Sun Crystal.

A tiny platform adventure

Pip vs. Kai

Kai stole the Sun Crystal and Brightwater Village is fading to grey. Collect treasure, sell it, gear up, and climb Kai's Tower.

Controls

←→
Move (or A / D)
Space
Jump (or ↑, W, Z). Hold for a higher jump.
E
Action (or X, ↓). Use doors, talk, plant, and throw sparks once you own the Spark Glove.
Esc
Pause. M toggles sound.

Things to do

  • Collect coins, berries, crystals, feathers and gems. Hit ? blocks from below.
  • Sell your finds at the Market and buy decorations for your home.
  • Upgrade at the Workshop: Spring Boots, Spark Glove, Coin Magnet, Heart Containers.
  • Grow Sunfruit in your home garden. Sleep after an adventure to make it grow.
  • Find the Star Shard in each world to open Kai's Tower, then beat Kai.
