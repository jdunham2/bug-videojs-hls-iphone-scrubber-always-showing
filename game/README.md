# Mischief Lane (vertical slice)

A top-down sandbox where you play a raccoon loose in a village.

## Run it
Open `game/index.html` in any browser. There's no build step and no dependencies.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | WASD / Arrow keys | Drag anywhere |
| Run | Hold Shift | Drag far |
| Sneak (slow, quiet) | Hold C | Drag gently |
| Grab / drop / pet an animal | E | "grab" / "drop" button |
| Throw (from a roof or treehouse: drop) | F | "throw" button |
| Eat what you're carrying | Q | "eat" button |
| Wear / take off a disguise (hat, bucket, pot, shirt, sock, crown) | G | "wear" button |
| Bury what you're carrying / dig it back up / dig for worms (empty paws) | B | "bury" / "dig up" button |
| Chitter (hop, scare butterflies, drop what's in your mouth) | Space | "chitter" button |
| Toggle minimap | M | — |
| Show / hide to-do list | T | "To-do" tab |
| Mute sound | N | — |

## What's in the game
- **Seasons and village kinds**: every daily village rolls one of four seasons and one of seventeen kinds.
  - *Spring*: blossom trees, drifting petals, Mrs. Fennick's tulip beds to trample, painted eggs hidden by bushes.
  - *Summer*: washing drying on lines (steal it), Rosa's lemonade on the picnic table.
  - *Autumn*: orange and red trees, falling leaves, chimney smoke, leaf piles to jump in (noisy) or hide in.
  - *Winter*: snow, bare and snowy trees, snow on roofs, scarves, falling snow, pawprints, frozen ponds and rivers
    you can slide across, snowmen to knock down, and E on the snow scoops a snowball to throw.
  - *Pond*, *riverside* (a river runs across the map; the road crosses on bridges; floating things drift
    downstream) or *seaside* (a beach and sea along the south edge, a pier, a rowing boat, seashells, Ollie's fish).
  - *Market*: market day, with four stalls on the plaza (Rosa's bakery, Bertram's fruit and veg, Ollie's fish,
    Percy's toys). The stallholders spend the morning behind their stalls shouting their wares, and there's a
    fruit cart to run into (apples everywhere).
  - *Windmill*: a windmill on the hill (its sails spin faster in the wind), flour sacks that burst into a cloud
    when thrown (walk through it and you come out white, and villagers who see you think you're a ghost), a
    wheat field to hide in, and a hay barn with a haystack to hide in.
  - *Lakeside*: a big lake with an island in the middle and a jetty. Press E by the rowing boat to climb in,
    row anywhere on the water, and E again to climb out onto the nearest land; villagers can't follow you to
    the island. There's also a fishing contest with a prize fish on the table.
  - *Castle*: old castle ruins in a moat, reached over a drawbridge: crumbling walls and towers, a bell tower
    (walk into the rope and the bell makes the whole village jump), suits of armour to hide in, and a heavy
    treasure chest in the courtyard.
  - *Forest*: extra-thick woods, two treehouses joined by a rope bridge (E at a ladder to climb up; left and
    right to cross; villagers never look up; F drops things on whoever's below), Bertram chopping wood by a
    woodpile you can send rolling, fairy rings of mushrooms (chitter inside one), and a hollow tree to hide in.
  - *Mountain*: a rocky slope with mountain goats that bound uphill when chased, boulders to roll down onto
    whoever's at the bottom, a cable car (E at the station) up to a lookout, and a cave where chitters echo.
  - *Canal*: a canal runs right across the village with a towpath and humpback bridges; hop aboard a
    narrowboat as it chugs along (E beside it), and pull the lock's red lever to drain or fill the lock
    (something glints in the mud when it's empty).
  - *Orchard*: rows of fruit trees (run into the trunk to shake down apples onto anyone underneath), a cider
    press that turns three apples into a jug of cider, and a hedge maze that's different every time.
  - *Railway*: a track across the village with a level crossing (the barriers come down and the lights
    flash as the train nears), a station with luggage on the platform, and a little steam train shuttling
    back and forth: hop on (E) when it stops at the station, throw things onto its roof, and don't stand on
    the tracks.
  - *Harbour*: a seaside town with a red-and-white lighthouse (climb it; its beam sweeps at dusk), lobster pots
    on the beach (a lobster pinches if you hold it too long), a fish market stall, and a fishing boat that
    sails in, unloads its catch at the end of the pier, and sails off again.
  - *Marsh*: a bog of sucking mud (slow and very muddy) with pools, boardwalks across it, stilt huts to hide
    in, a giant toad whose croak makes the whole village jump (and whose tongue snatches snacks), and a
    will-o'-the-wisp at dusk that leads you to a glowing pebble if you keep following it.
  - *School*: a schoolhouse with children out at playtime (they spot you and follow you about), a bell
    rope by the door that sends them all running inside, and a playground with swings, a slide and a
    roundabout (E to play).
  - *Hot springs*: steaming pools to soak in (swim into one), a bathhouse with fluffy towels on the rail,
    and a geyser that rumbles and then erupts, launching anything (or anyone) standing on it.
  - *Clifftop*: a seaside village with cliffs over the beach, a zipline from the clifftop down to the sand,
    a telescope, and a smugglers' cave in the cliff face with gold inside.
  - *Ski village* (always winter unless you pick a season): a piste with slalom flags, a chairlift to ride up
    (E at the bottom station) and a ski run back down (E at the top), skiers to knock over (bump into them or
    chitter as they pass), and a chalet whose counter has mugs of hot chocolate.
  - *Vineyard*: rows of vines heavy with grapes in summer and autumn, a grape-treading tub to hop about in
    (you leave purple pawprints after), a farm shop with jam and an honesty box, and a tractor that trundles
    up and down the road (hop on its trailer with E, but don't stand in front of it).
  - *Pleasure pier*: a seaside village with an arcade (keep trying the claw machine for a plushie, and nudge
    the penny falls for a bonus shiny, though the tilt alarm goes off), an ice-cream van with chimes, deckchairs
    with snoozing sunbathers (barge into one, or fiddle with it), and a Punch and Judy show to heckle (chitter)
    and to pinch Mr Punch's sausages from.
- **Shops**: Rosa's house is the bakery, Granny Mabel's the sweet shop and Percy's the post office. Each has a
  sign and awning outside, a doorbell that rings when you walk in, a counter of goods inside, and the
  shopkeeper spends part of the day behind it.
- **Mini-games**:
  - *Hide-and-seek*: stop next to Toby and he counts to ten, then comes looking (he checks the bushes). Stay
    hidden until he gives up.
  - *Race*: stand still on the RACE line on the road by the plaza; after the countdown, beat Biscuit to the
    FINISH banner (you'll need to run).
  - *Museum heist* (every castle village, sometimes elsewhere): treasures on plinths behind blinking red
    tripwires, and a guard on patrol. Touch a tripwire while it's on and the alarm goes off and the guard
    gives chase.
- **Pranks** (every village): E at a front door knocks, so run and hide before they answer; E at the plaza
  signpost swaps it round and confuses passers-by; whoopee cushions go where someone sits down; and a pot
  of pink paint turns any fence you walk along pink.
- **Catching little birds**: sparrows scatter if you walk or run up to them, but sneak (hold C, or push the
  stick gently) and you can creep right up and pounce (E). Robins, pigeons and blackbirds can be caught
  the same way. The bird sits in your paws for a moment, then flutters off unharmed.
- **Bandit, the rival raccoon**: a scruffy brown raccoon in a red bandana with a den (a tipped-over dustbin)
  at the other end of the road. All day he sneaks about stealing loose things and hauling them back to his
  den. Chitter at him and he drops whatever he's carrying, press E right next to him to snatch it out of his
  paws, or raid his den to take things back. Every minute or so a golden acorn turns up (look for the glinting
  beam, or the gold dot on the minimap) and it's a race: whoever gets it back to their own hideout first wins
  (+2 shinies for you). If you're carrying it he chases you and can snatch it, so run, hide, or chitter to make
  him let go. Race results are on the end-of-day card.
- **Pond and river life** (any village with open water, not in winter): a kingfisher that perches on the
  bank and dives for fish (sneak close to watch it), a beaver who hauls sticks to build up its dam (steal
  one and it slaps its tail at you), newts by the water's edge to sneak up on and catch, and a shy water
  vole that only pops out if you sit still near its bank.
- **Disguises and tricks**:
  - *Disguises*: hold a hat, bucket, pot, shirt, sock or the museum crown and press G to wear it. Villagers
    won't notice what you're carrying unless they get close ("Morning, stranger!"), but whoever owns the
    disguise will recognise their own hat.
  - *Burying*: B buries what you're carrying on grass, soil or sand, leaving a little mound; B on the mound
    digs it back up. Buried things go on your trophy shelf at the end of the day.
  - *Swimming*: walk into any pond, river, moat or sea (not while carrying something heavy). Villagers won't
    follow you in.
  - *Roofs*: E at the drainpipe on a house's front corner climbs onto the roof, where villagers never look.
    F drops things over the edge onto whoever is below, or down the chimney into the house if you're next
    to it.
- **The pest controller** (from day 2, every day from day 5): follows your trail around the village, chases
  you on sight, and sets three cage traps on the paths (step in one and you're stuck while he comes running;
  throw something at one to spring it safely). Make him jump and he drops his net.
- **Night raids** (some days from day 2): it's dark apart from lamplight and lit doorways. Most villagers are
  asleep in bed (walk, don't run, near them or they wake up shouting), and a night watchman patrols with a
  lantern and calls out the moment he sees you. The day ends at sunrise.
- **The trophy den**: everything in your hideout (and everything buried) at the end of each day goes on the
  trophy shelf in the den, kept across runs. Collections of themed things unlock new fur colours (ginger,
  river blue, candy, silver, midnight, arctic, golden), and there are achievements to earn.
- **Village events**: some days (about 2 in 5) there's something on, depending on the season:
  - *Village fete* (spring, summer): bunting, and a cake contest with three cakes and a first-prize rosette.
  - *Wedding* (spring to autumn): a flower arch, rows of chairs, confetti, a bouquet and a three-tier cake.
  - *Bonfire night* (autumn, winter): a bonfire, toffee apples, a box of rockets (throw one in the fire to set
    it off; every villager nearby jumps), and fireworks once dusk falls.
  - *Scarecrow festival* (summer, autumn): prize scarecrows around the village, waiting to be knocked over.
  - *Sports day* (spring, summer): a race lane with an egg-and-spoon race (run and the egg falls off) and a
    sack race (wear the sack with G and hop to the finish), a tug of war to sabotage with a chitter, and a
    gold medal on the podium.
  - *Funfair* (spring to autumn): a carousel and a big wheel to ride (E), a coconut shy (knock all three off for
    a prize teddy), a candyfloss stand, and a hall of mirrors whose reflections confuse anyone chasing you.
  - *Lantern festival* (spring, autumn, winter): strings of lanterns over the plaza that glow at dusk, dumpling
    and noodle stalls, a dragon dance winding round the plaza (follow close behind its tail to join in), and a
    table of sky lanterns: pick one up and let go, and it floats off into the sky.
  - *Zoo escape* (spring to autumn): the City Zoo van has broken down by the green and its animals are loose.
    Help the frantic zookeeper by sneaking up and pouncing (E): a monkey that swings between trees and pinches
    things (catch it when it comes down), a waddling penguin, a zebra that bolts if you don't sneak, and a
    kangaroo that boxes you if you come at it from the front.
  - Every village also has a hen house: hens panic and squawk when chased (loud enough to make villagers look)
    and their eggs can be stolen, eaten or smashed.
- **Weather**: each village also rolls weather that suits its season.
  - *Rain*: rain, a darker sky, puddles to splash through, villagers under umbrellas who see and hear less.
  - *Thunderstorm*: rain plus lightning; every thunderclap makes villagers jump (dropping things), panics the
    hens, sends the cat running and has Biscuit whimpering.
  - *Fog* (autumn, winter): thick mist with a clear patch around you; villagers can barely see.
  - *Snowstorm* (winter): heavy, wind-blown snow and poor visibility. *Windy*: thrown things drift off course.
  - *Hail* (spring, autumn): bouncing hailstones; villagers drop what they're doing and run home to shelter.
  - *Heatwave* (summer): a hazy, sweltering day; villagers move slowly and doze off on benches, and a dozing
    villager sees nothing (steal right from under their nose).
  - *Drizzle*: light rain that brings out the frogs. *Downpour*: torrential rain, huge spreading puddles, and
    villagers barely see or hear a thing; run through a puddle next to one to soak them.
  - *Whirlwind* (on windy days): a dust devil that wanders the village, flinging things into the air and
    sending you spinning if it catches you.
  - *Sleet* (spring, autumn, winter): half rain, half snow, and slippery paths.
  - *Blizzard* (winter): a near whiteout; the wind shoves you along and drifts pile up as it blows.
  - *Surprise snow* (late autumn, early spring): snow falling outside winter.
  - *Raining frogs* (rarely, in spring and summer): frogs fall from the sky and hop about, startling villagers.
  - *Lightning*: in a thunderstorm, bolts sometimes strike a tree near you, knocking things flying, making
    villagers jump and leaving the tree smoking; too close and you get zapped.
  - *Floods*: a downpour slowly spreads the pond out over the low ground around it; wading is slow, and
    things lying in the water drift about.
  - *Solar eclipse* (some days, announced on the morning card): for half a minute the sky goes dark as night
    and the villagers stop to stare.
  - *Rainy-day fun*: gusts snatch umbrellas out of villagers' hands (they chase after them, and you can grab
    them first), gutters spill onto anyone passing a house's front corner, everyone leaves muddy prints, and
    chittering in a puddle makes a splash that soaks anyone nearby.
  - *Gale* (autumn, spring, winter): it shoves you along and blows hats off villagers' heads; they chase
    after them, and you can grab them first.
  - *Sunshower* (spring, summer): sparkly golden rain in the sunshine, usually followed by a rainbow.
  - *Sky spectacles*: on clear evenings a shooting star sometimes falls nearby (pick it up to make a wish: bonus
    shinies, more time, or a burst of speed); in winter the northern lights ripple across the sky at dusk; and in
    spring and autumn a V of geese flies overhead.
  - *Moody clouds*: on dry days a little rain cloud may take a liking to you and follow you about (lead it over
    someone); mist rises off the water early in the day; and on frosty or snowy days the puddles freeze into
    slides.
  - *Frost* (autumn, winter): icy, slippery paths; villagers chasing you along them may slip and fall.
  - *Ice storm* (autumn, winter): freezing rain glazes the whole village, not just the paths, so everyone
    skids about, you included.
  - *Tornado* (spring, summer): a huge dark funnel that wanders the village, sucking in anything loose and
    throwing anyone (raccoon included) who gets too close.
  - *Cloudburst* (spring, summer): torrential rain that floods the roads; wading down them slows you (and
    everyone else) and sends up splashes.
  - *Breezy days*: in windy weather kites fly when you carry them, washing blows off the lines to be caught,
    and in spring and summer dandelion clocks grow in the grass (hold one and chitter to blow it).
  - The weather can change partway through the day; the morning card gives the forecast, and a toast tells you
    when it turns. When rain clears up, a rainbow appears with a pot of gold at its end (+3 shinies).
- **Animals**: ducks on the pond and river (they quack and flee, and eat any food dropped near them),
  sparrow flocks that scatter when you run at them (they shelter from bad weather), squirrels that dash up
  trees (and Biscuit barks up the tree, drawing villagers' eyes), frogs by the water in rain and spring, and
  seaside seagulls that swoop down and steal food out of your mouth (chitter to scare them off).
- **A bigger map**: every village is now 96×68 tiles (up from 72×52), with up to eight houses you can go
  into, more woods, bushes and hiding spots, and an extra minute on the clock to make up for the walking.
- **Woodland animals**: a wild boar sow with stripy piglets (she charges if you go near them), a moose that
  stands in the road like a wall until you chitter at it, bats that flit round the lamps after dusk, and a
  woodpecker that flies off tree to tree when you get close (follow it and it leads you to a stash of nuts).
- **Pets in the houses**: a goldfish bowl on someone's table (throw it and the goldfish flops about until it's
  rescued), Toby's hamster (walk up to the cage and out it pops, sending villagers shrieking), a kitten
  outside that follows you about once you've sat still beside it, and a budgie that squawks "BURGLAR!" if you
  pick something up in its room, sending its owner hurrying home.
- **The outskirts**: a campsite with two tents to hide in and a campfire (hold a marshmallow by it to toast
  it), a fenced scrapyard with junk piles to rummage through (E), and a field with scarecrows (their hats
  can be pinched) and crows to scatter.
- **Animal favours**: three animals with a "!" over their heads want something. The crow wants something shiny,
  the fox at its den by the forest edge wants an egg, and the mole wants three worms (press B on grass with
  empty paws to dig for them). Drop off what they want for 2 bonus shinies.
- **Farmyard extras**: a gaggle of geese that honk and chase you (and nip), a peacock that fans its tail if you
  stand still beside it, a donkey that brays when chittered at (and kicks if you walk behind it), and a turkey
  that puffs up and gobbles.
- **Town birds and pests**: pigeons on the plaza that mob anyone standing still with food, a magpie that swoops
  down to steal shiny things and carry them to its tree (scare it and it drops them), a wasp nest by the
  picnic table that sends a swarm after anyone carrying something sweet, and moths round the lamps at night.
- **Rainy-day critters**: snails come out in the rain (or in spring) leaving shiny trails and hide in their
  shells if you get close; worms pop up in wet grass and a blackbird swoops for them (grab one first); spider
  webs on the fences sparkle with dew in the rain, fog, frost and early morning; dragonflies dart over the
  water; and a ladybird lands on your nose if you stand still by a bush long enough.
- **River and seaside animals**: crabs on the beach pinch your toes (making you drop things), a seal lazes at
  the end of the pier and claps for a fish, otters play in the river or lake and swim circles round you if
  you swim with them, and a heron fishes at the water's edge and gobbles any fish left lying about.
- **The farm**: most villages have a fenced field with a gate and a trough. Sheep baa and bunch up when you run
  at them (herd them out through the gate), a cow moos and plods off, and a goat by the gate eats anything
  left lying around (newspapers, hats, washing) and charges if you chitter at it, headbutting you over.
- **Wild animals**: a fox that sneaks out of the woods to raid the hen house (run or chitter at it to chase it
  off), rabbits around the garden that bolt in zig-zags, a grumpy badger at dusk that charges if you get too
  close, and deer at the forest edge that you can only sneak up on by freezing whenever they look up.
- **Water and sky**: fish in the pond (wait quietly at the edge and one may leap into your paws), a swan that
  hisses and chases you off, a beehive by the garden in spring and summer (bump it and a swarm chases you;
  lead it to a villager, or hide in a bush to lose it, and grab the honey), and butterflies you can catch
  if you walk rather than run.
- **Pets and pests**: a parrot on a perch that repeats what villagers say and shouts "THIEF!" if you carry
  someone's things past it, a pig in a mud wallow (roll in it and you leave muddy pawprints everywhere, even
  indoors, until a puddle or the rain washes you off), mice indoors that send villagers shrieking if you
  chase one at them, and Pepper, a yappy terrier who gives you away until you win her over with a snack.
- **Winter animals**: robins on the fences (walk up quietly), a white stoat that sneaks into the hen house
  and runs off with an egg (scare it and it drops it), a snowy owl in the trees, and a reindeer pulling a
  sleigh of presents along the road (startle it and a present falls off).
- **Year-round animals**: a horse by its stable (feed it, but never walk behind it), a mother duck and her
  ducklings (walk up slowly and they follow you instead; run and they scurry back to mum, who chases you,
  quacking), a llama that spits at whoever annoys it (or whoever is nearest), and Granny Mabel's tortoise,
  which plods about and can be carried off.
- **Snow fun** (winter): a sledging hill (E by the sledge to ride it; crash into villagers at the bottom),
  three snowballs dropped together make your own snowman, Toby starts a snowball fight when he spots you
  (hit him three times to win), snowdrifts to burrow into, an igloo to hide in, and something thrown hard at
  the front of a house knocks off the icicles and dumps the roof snow on anyone by the door. Stand still
  and look up while it's snowing to catch a snowflake on your tongue.
- **At dusk** (the last part of the day, not in winter): windows light up, hedgehogs snuffle out from the
  bushes and curl into a ball when startled, an owl on a tree hoots an alarm if it spots you, and fireflies
  drift about to be caught.
- **Runs ("days")**: every day is a freshly generated village (roads, plaza, pond, garden and houses shuffled
  around), a hand of tasks from a pool of 318 (filtered to what the season and village allow), a clock until sunset (the world warms and dims as it runs out) and a
  few strikes before you're out. Harder days ask for more and villagers get faster and sharper-eyed.
- **The den**: shinies from tasks (plus time and day bonuses on a win) buy upgrades that carry over between days:
  Quick Paws, Strong Back, Light Step, Big Chitter, Thick Fur, Long Day, Slippery. Progress is saved in your browser.
  Losing a day resets the streak to Day 1, but upgrades stay.
- **Houses you can go into**: push up into a front door to go in, walk out over the mat to leave. Every house has a
  furnished room (bed, fireplace or stove, shelves, wardrobe, table, rug) with things on the table and lying around.
  Villagers go home during their rounds, so a house isn't always empty, and they'll chase you round their own kitchen.
- **Seven villagers**: Bertram (gardener), Mrs. Fennick (neighbour), Rosa (picnicker), Toby (her son), Percy (postman,
  fast), Granny Mabel (knits on her doorstep, slow) and Ollie (fisherman). Each has a daily round worked out from the
  village layout, owns certain things, and will chase you if they see you carrying them. Vision is a cone, blocked by
  houses and walls. Caught? They snatch it back, shove you, and you lose a strike.
- **~105 things to grab per village** (about a third indoors): pies, cakes, teapots, teddy bears, cookie jars, crops,
  crates, a wheelbarrow, parcels, milk bottles, a kite, a fishing rod, yarn, gnomes... Light things go in your mouth,
  heavy ones overhead. Throw them, sink them, float them, or stash them in your hideout.
- **Hiding bushes**: big walk-in shrubs (about 18 per village, many beside houses and the garden). Inside one,
  villagers can't see you unless they walk right into it; your eyes peek out and a "Hidden" pill shows. You can't
  hide carrying something heavy, and running makes the leaves rustle. Duck in mid-chase and chasers lose track.
- **Biscuit the dog** (Bertram's): wanders near his bed and sniffs you out (bushes don't fool his nose), barks so
  villagers turn to look, and knocks things out of your mouth to carry back to his bed. Throw something near him
  and he fetches it instead. Press E next to him to pet him and he follows you around as a friend for a while.
  He guards his bone. The Dog Treats upgrade makes him start each day friendly.
- **Hiding under beds**: crawl under any bed from its foot end. Same rules as bushes; a lump appears under the
  blanket and your eyes peek out. Villagers walk round beds, so they won't find you unless they bump into you.
- **Marmalade the cat** (Mrs. Fennick's): ignores you. Naps in cosy spots (armchair, bench, doorstep, garden),
  wanders through doors, and knocks things off tables. Biscuit chases her on sight, which keeps him off your back;
  chitter at her and she hisses and bolts, so you can herd her toward him. Pet her while she naps for a purr.
- **Fragile things** (vases, teapots, frames, cookie jars, milk bottles) smash if they hit the floor hard, whether
  you threw them or the cat swatted them. Nearby villagers turn to see what broke.
- **Eating people's food**: carrying pie, cake, cookies, a sandwich, bread, cheese, an apple, a carrot, cabbage,
  a pumpkin or milk? Press Q to eat it, bite by bite (stand still; it shrinks each bite). Your tummy fills (bar in
  the run bar) and slowly digests. Overeat and you're Stuffed: rounder, slower, no running. Sweets give a 12 s
  sugar rush. Munching is noisy (nearby villagers turn to look), Biscuit smells food from farther away, and owners
  who watch you finish their food are outraged.
- **Hiding in wardrobes**: stand in front of one and press E to climb in (not with anything heavy); move or press E
  to step out. Nobody can see you in there, but a villager who watched you climb in will open the doors.
- **Online** (when opened from the claude.ai link; offline play is unchanged):
  - *Play together live*: villages are daily (the same village for everyone on the same day number today, UTC),
    and each village has a live room. Friends in it show up as raccoons with name tags, colour scarves and minimap
    dots; you see them move, carry things, eat, chitter and hide. Only raccoons are shared: each player's villagers
    and items are their own.
  - *Leaderboard*: best day reached and lifetime shinies, shown in the den. Saving a score needs Contributor
    access (or ownership) on the artifact.
- **Hideout**: a hollow log at one end of the road. Things in its nest are safe, and villagers won't follow you in.

## Code layout (`game.js`)
- **Map**: `buildMap()` lays out tiles and places objects (seeded RNG, same village every time)
- **Items**: `ITEM` table (name, size, heavy, floats, draw), `placeItems()`, `updateItems()` physics, `grabOrDrop()` / `throwHeld()`
- **Village generator**: `genLayout()` + `buildMap()` (roads, plaza, pond, garden, houses, carved paths),
  `newVillage(seed)` rebuilds everything for a run
- **Interiors**: `buildInteriors()` puts a room per house in a hidden strip below the map; `areaOf()`, doors in
  `updateDoors()`, villagers cross doors with `travel()`
- **Villagers**: `VILLAGER_DEFS` (looks, speeds, lines), `stopsFor()` (daily round from the layout),
  `updateVillagers()` state machine (route → act, chase, fetch, return, sulk, startled), A* `findPath()`, `canSee()`
- **Hiding & dog**: `makeHideBush()`, `updateHiding()`, `canSeePlayer()`; `spawnDog()`, `updateDog()` state machine
  (sit, wander, alert, chase, catchase, fetch, return, happy, follow); `spawnCat()`, `updateCat()` (nap, stroll,
