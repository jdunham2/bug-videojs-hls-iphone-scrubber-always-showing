DEAD ORBIT PROTOCOL

"We have lost everything and Coalight has hold of us. I am declaring Dead Orbit Protocol."Captain Hale, to all hands

Six hours into the Coalight system, the Emberflight goes dark. Reactor, air, shields, sensors and drive all shut down at once, and the ship is left stranded in a decaying orbit. You are Rook, engineer's mate, and the engineering deck is yours.

Bring her back one system at a time. Each time something comes back, report it to the Captain: a repaired system stays off the board until the bridge has heard it from you.

  1. Bus breakers
  2. Reactor
  3. Life support
  4. Shields
  5. Sensors
  6. Main drive, then burn clear
