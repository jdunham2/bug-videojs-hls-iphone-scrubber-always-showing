You arrive in Port Wren with a pack, a few coins and no name on the road. Nine settlements lie scattered across the coast, the pine woods, the frozen north, the eastern sands, the Gloomwood and the smoking Cinderlands. Gather, craft, take on work, haggle, and face the six great beasts that rule the wild.

The Guildmistress grants a Charter of the Free Road to any trader who earns 600 coin and forms pacts with four settlements.
