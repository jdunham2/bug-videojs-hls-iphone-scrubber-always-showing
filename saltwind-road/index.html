You arrive in Port Wren with a pack, a few coins and no name on the road. Thirteen settlements lie scattered from the salt coast and pine woods to the jade jungle, the red canyons, the haunted moor and the stormy north-east cliffs. Gather, craft, take on work, haggle, and face the ten great beasts that rule the wild. Buy a homestead, farm the land, and ride out with a pet at your side.

The Guildmistress grants a Charter of the Free Road to any trader who earns 600 coin and forms pacts with four settlements.
