# Brindle Isle art credits

## Characters (hero, Oma Fen, Tobin, Farmer Pell, Captain Marla, Brother Hob, Nell, Ysolde, Old Bjorn, Zahir, Amara, Captain Rosa, Mother Moss, Brakka the Smith, Aeris the Windkeeper)
From the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) (Liberated Pixel Cup).
Layers: body, head, hair, long-sleeve shirt, pants, shoes, arming sword (slash and backslash), axe, pickaxe and plate and leather armour (recolored); animations walk, run, idle, combat idle, sit, emote, jump, slash, backslash, thrust, spellcast and hurt. Colors were adjusted for this game.

Authors: Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), bluecarrot16, Durrani, Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), Evert, JaidynReiman, Joe White, Johannes Sjölund (wulax), Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Matthew Krohn (makrohn), MuffinElZangano, Nila122, Pierre Vigier (pvigier), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), TheraHedwig, Tuomo Untinen (reemax).

Licenses: CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0 (see the generator's CREDITS.csv for per-file details).

## Tiles, trees, animals, monsters, dungeon tiles and items
[Kenney](https://kenney.nl) Tiny Town, Tiny Battle, Tiny Farm and Tiny Dungeon packs, CC0 (public domain).

## Hand-drawn
The pixel rock, crystal and Slime King crown sprites, the temple and crypt entrances, crystal switches, furniture and village props (beds, hearths, wells, lamps, boats, market stalls, cacti, palm trees, ruins and more), the Sand Wyrm, the pyramid entrance, spike traps, sunbeams and sun-balls, giant mushrooms, bogs, rafts, the Rotting Hollow, the Bog Hag and the Rotroot, the Molten Forge, the Forgemaster, the Magma Drake, lava, flame vents, obsidian, the forge, the sky balloon, floating islands, cloud bridges, jet streams, the updraft, the windmill, storm puffs, gale hawks, windlings, cloud sentinels, the shroomling, toad, wisp, magmite, ember imp, boulderback and salamander creatures and a few inventory icons were drawn for this game.

## Rebuilding
Edit `src/game.js` or `src/shell.html`, then run `python3 build.py` to regenerate the self-contained `index.html`.
