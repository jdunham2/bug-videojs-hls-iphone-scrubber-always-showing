# Brindle Isle art credits

## Characters (hero, Oma Fen, Tobin)
From the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) (Liberated Pixel Cup).
Layers: body, head, hair, long-sleeve shirt, pants, shoes, arming sword, axe and pickaxe. Colors were adjusted for this game.

Authors: Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), bluecarrot16, Durrani, Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), Evert, JaidynReiman, Joe White, Johannes Sjölund (wulax), Manuel Riecke (MrBeast), Matthew Krohn (makrohn), MuffinElZangano, Nila122, Pierre Vigier (pvigier), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), TheraHedwig, Tuomo Untinen (reemax).

Licenses: CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0 (see the generator's CREDITS.csv for per-file details).

## Tiles, trees, animals, slimes and items
[Kenney](https://kenney.nl) Tiny Town, Tiny Battle, Tiny Farm and Tiny Dungeon packs, CC0 (public domain).

## Hand-drawn
The pixel rock and crystal sprites and a few inventory icons were drawn for this game.

## Rebuilding
Edit `src/game.js` or `src/shell.html`, then run `python3 build.py` to regenerate the self-contained `index.html`.
