Timberhold

You are alone in a clearing with an axe. Every night the forest sends more against you: wolves and snakes, then crows that fly over walls, boars, cougars, bears and moose. A boss leads each night from the second on, and every map has its own line-up. Chop trees, quarry stone and raise a house around yourself before dark. A walled room with a door and a bed, still standing after the fifth night, wins.

Low is smoother on phones and older computers.

        How to play
        WASD / arrows
        Move. On a phone, drag anywhere to move.
        Mouse / Space
        Use the weapon in hand. Only the axe chops trees and rocks; rocks also give iron.
        Craft menu (J)
        Opens pets, rides and every recipe you have the ingredients for. Close it with its Close button, J or Esc. I hides the weapon and build bars for a clear view; their keys still work.
        Weapon bar
        Forty-three weapons, from spear to solar lance; scroll the bar sideways to see them all. Click a weapon to craft it, then to equip it. Tab or the mouse wheel switches. R makes arrows, F makes bullets.
        The world
        Twice as big as before, with lakes (and lava in the Ashlands) you have to walk around. The map in the top-right corner shows you, your friends, your buildings and the bosses.
        Your house
        A finished house can be furnished. A workbench, forge, pantry and trophy wall only work inside it, each adds a house level, and each gives a bonus: cheaper builds and weapons, walls that repair at dawn, stronger food, and more damage for every boss you have beaten. At level 5 the walls take 30% less damage. The arrow-slit wall works as a window you can shoot out of.
        More builds
        Iron door, arrow-slit wall (you can shoot through it, animals cannot pass), shock fence, ballista, landmine, fire grate, scarecrow (keeps flying animals away), torch post, and a garden plot and beehive that grow herbs, berries and honey over time.
        Mining
        Coloured rocks are veins: rust is iron, yellow is gold, blue is crystal, black is coal. Coal and sulfur make powder rounds and dynamite. Gold makes a golden axe and plate armor; crystal and gold make a ward that slows animals.
        Pick-ups
        New finds: berry bushes, honeycombs, resin, sulfur crystals, and wool from sheep. Also mushrooms (a little health), feathers (cheap arrows), bones (bone fence, pet charm) and gems. Walk over things to take them: herb bushes (Z makes a bandage from 2 herbs), scrap piles (iron), supply crates, and hides dropped by animals, which make boots, a vest and the sling.
        Pets and rides
        You can keep three pets; click one you own to let it go. The balloon floats over walls and trees where only flying animals reach you, but it cannot fly at night, and the tank fires its own gun. The top bar buys companions with meat (dog, hawk, war bear) that fight beside you, and rides (horse, buggy, armored truck). B gets on or off your ride; the buggy and truck ram animals.
        Night rewards
        Every night you survive brings a gift and unlocks something new: traps, iron walls, armor (V), turrets, grenades (H to make, G to throw) and a machine gun.
        1 – 9, 0, L, X
        The crystal ward and bone fence have no key; click them. Pick a build piece, then click a nearby tile. Q returns to your weapon.
        E · C · P · M · K
        Eat meat to heal · craft a flint axe · pause · mute · save. The game also saves itself every dawn.
