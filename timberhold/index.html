Timberhold

You are alone in a clearing with an axe. Every night the forest sends wolves, then boars, then bears. Chop trees, quarry stone and raise a house around yourself before dark. A walled room with a door and a bed, still standing after the fifth night, wins.

WASD / arrows
Move. On a phone, drag anywhere to move.
Mouse / Space
Use the weapon in hand. Only the axe chops trees and rocks; rocks also give iron.
Weapon bar
Click a weapon to craft it, then to equip it. Tab or the mouse wheel switches. R makes arrows, F makes bullets.
